ScanShift

Claude Impact Lab NYC, Track 3 (Radiology / Oncology)

For the radiologist reading a cancer patient's follow-up chest film. ScanShift compares the prior and current image and drafts a structured first read: what changed, what did not, what to look at first, and what it declines to call. A resident reviews it and an attending signs it. Only then does the result go to the ordering provider.

Safety design
  • The reason for the scan is hidden from the blind reads.
  • The blind read runs twice. The app shows agreement between the reads, not a model-reported confidence.
  • The model gives no measurements. A radiologist enters them and code does the arithmetic.
  • "Can't determine" is a first-class output with a reason and a next step.
  • Nothing leaves radiology before the attending signs.
